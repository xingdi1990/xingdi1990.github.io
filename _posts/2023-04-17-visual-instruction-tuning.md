LLaVA (Large Language and Vision Assistant) pioneers visual instruction tuning — the first systematic attempt to adapt language-only instruction-following to the multimodal domain. By converting image-text pairs into an instruction-following format using GPT-4 generated data, the authors create a general-purpose visual assistant that can reason about images, answer questions, and follow complex instructions. The paper demonstrates that language-only instruction data alone is insufficient, and that multimodal instruction data is key to building capable vision-language assistants.
